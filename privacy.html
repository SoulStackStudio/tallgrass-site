Tallgrass

Privacy Policy · updated 12 November 2026

Who we are

Tallgrass is a free, fan-made game made by the team behind the YouTube channel Glowbugs TV. This page covers the game, our Discord bot Glo, and Glow, the private tool we use to run our own channel. We never sell your data.

The game

What we keep

When you sign in with Discord we keep your Discord ID and username, your trainer name, and your game save: your Pokémon, items and progress. This lives on our game server so your account works on every device and online features like trading and raids work.

Supporters

If you choose a support tier, your payment is handled by Paystack. We never see or store your card details; we only keep your support tier and whether it's active.

Glo, our Discord bot

What Glo does

Glo welcomes new members, gives out roles, keeps supporter perks in sync with the game, and helps answer questions in help tickets in #get-help.

What it keeps

A join and leave log (username, ID and time), kept for 90 days, and a record of each help ticket (username, ID and topic). Glo doesn't keep copies of ticket conversations, and it doesn't store the text of messages outside help tickets. Supporter sync sends only your Discord ID and tier to our own game server.

Help tickets

To write a reply, Glo sends the recent text and screenshots of a help ticket to Anthropic's API (Claude). Anthropic is the only outside service that sees ticket content, and it isn't used to train AI. Ticket threads are only visible to you and our team.

Glow and YouTube

What Glow is

Glow is a private desktop tool we use for our own YouTube channel. It isn't offered to the public. It uses YouTube API Services to read our own channel's statistics and to upload videos we've made to our own channel, with their title, description and publish time.

What it accesses and keeps

Glow only ever accesses our own channel. It doesn't access anyone else's channel or personal data. Statistics and sign-in tokens are stored only on our own computer, never shared with or sent to anyone else, and can be deleted at any time.

Google and YouTube

Glow's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your choices

Removing your data

Want your data removed, or have a question about privacy? Open a ticket in #get-help on our Discord and we'll sort it out.

Security

Keys and passwords for our services are kept in private files, never in public code, and access to stored data is limited to our team.
