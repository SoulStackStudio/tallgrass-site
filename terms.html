Playing Tallgrass

An online game

Tallgrass is a free, fan-made online game. Your account, your Pokémon and the results you earn live on our server, not just on your computer.

Play it as it comes

Please don't edit your save file or game data, run a changed (modded) copy of the game, or use bots or automation to play for you.

No exploiting bugs

If you find a bug that gives an unfair advantage, tell us in the Discord instead of using it. Honest reports are welcome (and sometimes rewarded).

Fair play is checked on the server

The server decides your Pokémon, their looks and odds, and checks results. Numbers that can't have come from honest play may be rolled back.

Accounts

Accounts that break these terms can be suspended or removed. We'll always aim to be fair, and server results are final.

Have fun

That's really it. Hunt, trade, raid and enjoy it. Questions? Ask in the Discord.
