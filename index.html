Everything in the tall grass

Raids 2.0

Eight bosses up at once: Legendaries, a Mythical, a Mega raid and more. Bring your best squad, team up with real trainers near your level, and earn Raid Eggs on every raid, won or lost. Hit 50,000 damage for a Master Ball.

New in 0.51

All 1,025 Pokémon

Every species across eight islands, plus Megas, regional forms and Alphas. Fill the Pokédex and your stamp book.

Shiny, Void, Prismatic

Three tiers of rare. The shadowy Void and the rainbow Prismatic are ones you won't find anywhere else.

Day Care breeding

Pair them up, fill the egg basket and hatch for perfect IVs and rare looks. A Hearth Pokémon keeps the eggs warm.

Friends & trades

Add friends by code, trade one-on-one, or throw a spare into Wonder Trade and get a surprise from another trainer.

Buddy Pokémon

Pick a Buddy to walk behind you on the route. It finds Candy and items, and earns hearts all the way to Best Buddy.

Trainer profiles

Your trainer card with ribbons, titles and favourite Pokémon. Climb the Trainers board and visit your friends' PCs.

Classic or Modular

One big window with tabs, or floating windows you place anywhere. Switch any time in Settings.

Updates itself

New versions download in the background. Download once and you're always on the latest.

Recommended for you

Windows

169 MB · Windows 10 or 11 · zip

Download for Windows
  1. Unzip it anywhere you like (right-click → Extract All).
  2. Open the folder and double-click Tallgrass.exe. No install needed.
  3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. It's just because the game isn't from a big publisher.
Recommended for you

Mac

142 MB · Apple Silicon (M1–M4) · zip

Download for Mac
  1. Unzip, then drag Tallgrass.app into Applications.
  2. It's not from the App Store, so the first time only, open Terminal, paste this and press Enter:
    xattr -cr /Applications/Tallgrass.app && codesign --force --deep --sign - /Applications/Tallgrass.app
  3. Open Tallgrass from Applications. After that it opens normally.

Or play in your browser

No download: open Tallgrass in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer and log in with Discord. Already playing on your PC? Choose Import a save instead and pick your save.json (press Win+R, open %APPDATA%\Glowbugs Hunt).

One device plays at a time: opening it on another device pauses the first. Saves don't sync between devices yet (cloud save is next), so moving to the browser means playing there from now on. Phones are coming soon.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Tallgrass is a free fan project, with no ads and nothing to buy. Everything in the game is earned by playing.

Windows says it's from an unknown publisher. Is that OK?

That warning shows for any game that isn't signed by a big company. Click More info then Run anyway. You only need to do it once.

Will it slow my PC down?

It's made to idle all day next to whatever you're doing. If it ever crashes or stutters, turn off Hardware acceleration in Settings.

Does it need the internet?

The hunt works offline. Friends, trades, Wonder Trade, Raid helpers and the Trainers board need you online.

How do updates work?

New versions download in the background while you play. When one is ready you'll see it up top, and one click restarts you on it. Your save carries over.

Where is my save?

On your computer. On Windows it's %APPDATA%\Glowbugs Hunt\save.json. Back it up any time.

Intel Mac, Linux, phone?

Right now it's Windows 10/11 and Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer). Ask in the Discord if you'd like another platform.

Is this an official Pokémon game?

No. It's a fan-made game, free and not for sale, and not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures or The Pokémon Company.
